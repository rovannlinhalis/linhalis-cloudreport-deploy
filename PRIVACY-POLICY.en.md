# Privacy Policy — CloudReport Community

**Effective: October 18, 2026**

This policy separates data kept in your installation from data required to activate the Community license.

## 1. Locally stored data

During normal Community operation, Linhalis does not receive passwords, local accounts, projects, data sources, integration credentials, report definitions, files, or generated results. This data remains in the infrastructure operated by you. The installation operator is responsible for protection, legal basis, retention, backups, and data-subject requests.

CloudReport Community does not include Linhalis product telemetry or advertising. The operator's infrastructure may retain its own technical logs according to its configuration.

## 2. Activation data

To issue and maintain the free license, the activation form sends Linhalis:

- the registrant's name and email;
- organization, when provided;
- the technical installation identifier;
- the configured instance name and API URL;
- the accepted terms version and activation date.

This data is used exclusively to create and maintain the license contract, validate the installation, prevent abuse, and communicate license-related matters. It is not sold or used for behavioral advertising.

## 3. Sharing and processors

Activation data passes through Linhalis's licensing automation and is stored in Linhalis's contract system. Infrastructure providers may process it under security and confidentiality obligations. Linhalis may disclose it when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

## 4. Retention and security

Activation data is retained while the contract is active and for as long as necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, and legal claims. Technical and organizational safeguards proportionate to the risk are applied, but no system is completely immune to incidents.

## 5. Rights and contact

The registrant may request access, correction, or deletion of activation data, subject to information that must be retained for legal obligations or legal claims. Use the channels published at [linhalis.com](https://linhalis.com/). Deleting the technical association may prevent future license validation.

## 6. Changes

This policy may be updated for legal, security, or service changes. The current version will be identified in the activation flow and this document.
